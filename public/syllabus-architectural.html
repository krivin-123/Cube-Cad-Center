Architectural Design

Master the art and science of architectural drafting and 3D modeling. Learn to translate conceptual designs into precise, construction-ready blueprints and immersive BIM models.

architecture

AutoCAD • Revit • SketchUp

Module 1: 2D Drafting with AutoCAD

  • Introduction to AutoCAD Interface and Navigation
  • Drawing and Editing Commands for Floor Plans
  • Layer Management and Properties
  • Dimensioning, Text, and Hatching
  • Creating and Using Blocks
  • Plotting and Layouts for Professional Output

Module 2: 3D Conceptual Modeling with SketchUp

  • Push/Pull Mechanics and 3D Forms
  • Groups, Components, and the Outliner
  • Materials and Textures
  • Lighting, Shadows, and Scenes
  • V-Ray Integration for Photorealistic Rendering

Module 3: Building Information Modeling with Revit

  • Understanding BIM vs. CAD
  • Creating Walls, Doors, Windows, and Roofs
  • Families and Parametric Components
  • Generating Sections, Elevations, and Schedules
  • Collaboration and Worksharing

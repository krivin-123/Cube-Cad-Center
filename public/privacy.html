Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Data Fiduciary: Cube Cad Center

1. Overview & Data Fiduciary Notice

This Privacy Policy explains how Cube Cad Center ("we", "our", or "us"), collects, processes, and protects the digital personal data of prospective students, enrolled learners, and site visitors ("you" or "Data Principal") in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023).

For any queries regarding this policy, the designated Data Fiduciary contact is reachable at info@cubecadcenter.co.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you request a counseling appointment, demo class, or syllabus details, we collect only the minimum necessary information:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number (10 digits), and Email Address (optional).
  • Academic & Career Interests: Selected CAD/BIM course or counseling program, preferred branch (Satellite or Bopal campus), and consultation mode.
  • Appointment & Communication Logs: Preferred date and time slot, notes provided by you, and records of our advisory calls or messages.
  • Consent Verification: Timestamp and digital affirmation of your consent to be contacted.

3. Purpose of Processing (Lawful Basis)

We process your personal data strictly for specified, legitimate purposes based on your explicit consent:

  • Scheduling and confirming your 1-on-1 counseling session, demo class, or campus visit.
  • Dispatching appointment notifications, reminders, calendar invites, and status updates via Email or SMS.
  • Providing course syllabus documents, batch timings, and fee structures relevant to your inquiry.
  • Managing admissions records and telecaller follow-up services requested by you.

4. Rights of Data Principals (DPDP Act 2023)

As a Data Principal under Indian law, you possess enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access Information: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You can request correction of inaccurate data or deletion of your consultation records when no longer required.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer, following which we will discontinue communication.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to expeditious resolution of privacy-related grievances.
  • Right to Nominate: You may nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of incapacity.

5. Data Retention & Security Measures

Your personal data is protected using role-based access control, encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), parameterized queries to prevent data injection, and strictly masked logging (phone numbers and emails are never logged in full). We retain inquiry data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the counseling purpose or as required under applicable statutory regulations.

6. No Sale or Unauthorized Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party commercial marketing agencies. Data is accessible solely to authorized admissions counselors and technical communication providers (e.g. TRAI-registered SMS aggregators and transactional email relays) under strict data protection obligations.
